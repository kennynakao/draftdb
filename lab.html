1 · Sequential redraft

Each player can only be taken once

The team pages compared every real pick with the model's best player still on the board, independently per pick, so one high-WAR player could be the model's answer at ten different slots. Here the real draft is replayed in pick order and the team on the clock takes the model's highest-ranked player still available. Same 385 players, different slots, so the league total is unchanged; what moves is which slots hold the WAR.

2 · Pick-value weighting

A swap at picks 2 and 12 is not a swap at 40 and 45

Spearman treats every rank swap alike. The empirical pick-value curve (isotonic fit of 5-season WAR on overall pick, 2000–2018 classes) gives each slot a weight; the value of an order is the slot-weighted WAR, scaled so the perfect order is 100 and the reverse order 0. 3 · Early-career WAR is noisy, and the noise is not neutral

The real draft looks better the longer you wait

On the 2007–2018 classes, where five seasons are complete, the real draft order correlates with first-season WAR at 0.07 and with five-season WAR at 0.32. Teams draft for years three to five; a model trained on a front-weighted label (0.85 discount per season) is judged on the seasons it was built to predict. Classes 2023–2025 are scored on one to three seasons, which favours the model.

5 · Undrafted players

Where the gems would have landed

The vault scores drafted players only. The test files also hold the undrafted players who later reached the NBA, plus early entrants who stayed in, so the engine built here can place them on the board. Caveat: that undrafted set was assembled knowing who made the NBA, so it is enriched for success and the expanded-pool numbers are not a clean benchmark.

5b · The clean universe: who was actually in the pool

Rule. A player is in the universe of draft Y if, before draft night, he was (a) on the league's final early-entry list for Y (the NBA's initial list minus every withdrawal release, reconciled to the league's stated counts: nba.com and pr.nba.com releases, captured 2026-10-20), college or international; or (b) a Division-I senior in season Y with at least 40% of his team's minutes and 10 games (Torvik), removing seniors who played Division-I basketball again in Y+1 (eligibility left, so not in that year's pool); or (c) a EuroLeague or EuroCup player listed at 22, the automatic international age, who had been in no earlier class and had no NBA record. Every drafted player is in by definition (the draft enumerates them; the few outside (a)–(c), G League Ignite and the like, are counted). Undrafted players enter only through (a)–(c): nothing about who later reached the NBA is used, and Torvik's post-draft pick column is never read. The test files' undrafted rows that the rule does not reach are dropped. Inputs: the v4.22 row where one exists, else a row rebuilt from Torvik, the RSCI lists and the Torvik-context builder (verified column by column against real rows); EuroLeague rows from the league exports. The strict engine (train 2007–2018, base recipe, fold-learned weights) scores the pool; nothing was selected on 2019–2025. Teams: every undrafted player at pick 61.

6 · Engine experiments

Does anything move the pick-value metric?

A compact re-implementation of the pipeline (data v4.22, the champion's input families, international-line mask, shrinkage, shoot/prod features, Gaussian-rank label, ridge-on-top-100 + quantile XGBoost, fold-learned rank weights) run on this Mac. Selection is on the seven season-consistent walk-forward folds 2012–2018 only; the strict and expanding columns are one blind look each, reported for every variant, never used to choose.

7 · Robustness

Alternate outcomes, mean Spearman over 7 classes

WAR floored at zero removes the penalty for high picks who played a lot on bad teams; minutes reward whoever got on the floor, which follows draft slot, so the teams lead there by construction.

Methods and limits

Data. Public repo answers (WAR per season through 2025–26), draft history from stats.nba.com, the identity file for names, data v4.22 inputs for the engine. Horizon per class: 2019–2021 five seasons, 2022 four, 2023 three, 2024 two, 2025 one. Players who never played score 0.

Boards. Current model strict from model/board_current_2019_2025.csv; the expanding board reconstructed from the team-card audit ranks (385 of 392 drafted players); Colin's strict board parsed from his README tables (393 of 413 names matched).

Pick-value curve. Isotonic (non-increasing) regression of 5-season WAR on overall pick for drafted players 2000–2018, lightly smoothed, floored at zero. Pick 1 ≈ 21 WAR, pick 10 ≈ 8, pick 30 ≈ 5, pick 50 ≈ 2.

Engine. TabICL was omitted from the queue runs (15 s per fit on CPU); the XGBoost + ridge pair reproduces the box's own ablation of that pair (strict 0.44 vs 0.395, expanding 0.47 vs 0.475). Fold objectives average horizons k = 1..5 as the box does.

Noise. One class's Spearman has a standard error near 0.13; a seven-class mean about 0.05. Differences of two or three points between boards are inside that.
