Model Runs

We re-ran every draft since 2019 with a tabular model using everything publicly knowable before draft night — college and international box scores, measurements, recruiting and scouting-report grades, and the final mock-draft consensus. Then we checked whose order better matched how the players actually turned out: the model, or the NBA teams that made the real picks. Players are ranked by WAR, the wins they were worth over the NBA seasons they have played so far.

Order accuracy: how closely a draft order matches the players' actual WAR ranking (Spearman rank correlation as a percentage). 100% is a perfect order, 0% is no relationship. Averaged over the seven test drafts only.

How it was tested #

Training: 2010–2018. Test: 2019–2025. The model is trained once on the 2010–2018 classes — every drafted player plus every undrafted player who reached the NBA — and never sees a test class. Every test draft is scored on the NBA seasons its players have completed so far: 2019 to 2021 on five seasons, 2022 on four, 2023 on three, 2024 on two, 2025 on one. Model selection used walk-forward folds inside the training window only (train on 2010 to y−1, predict class y). Test outcomes sit in a hash-locked vault; every look at them is written to an append-only ledger. Nothing from a draft's own year or later is ever visible to the model.

Runs #

Per class, shipped model #

Value of the order = WAR delivered by each draft slot, weighted linearly by slot, scaled so the perfect order is 100% and the worst is 0% (random = 50%). Shipped model: XGBoost + TabICL rank stack on data v3. Every formula, every permutation tried and the data rebuild are documented in the repository: github.com/Spoofyy-1/draftdb (docs/METHODS.md, docs/EXPERIMENTS.md).
