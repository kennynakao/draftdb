// Caveats //

Read this honestly

  • WAR to date is counted over at most a player’s first five NBA seasons. It is a running total, not a career, and it says nothing about what either player does next.
  • The recent classes are scored on one or two seasons. Those cards will move, and some of them will flip, as more seasons are played.
  • A model rank is a within-class ranking of drafted players only. It is an ordering of that year’s board, not a grade against every player in basketball, and a player with no rank was simply not scored.
  • The model’s choice is its pick in a sequential redraft: the draft replayed in pick order with each player taken once, from a board built only from what was knowable before the draft. It is not a claim that anyone knew how the careers would turn out.
  • Early-career WAR rewards early producers. On the complete 2007–2018 classes the real draft order correlates 0.07 with first-season WAR and 0.32 with five-season WAR; teams draft for years three to five, so cards for the 2023–2025 classes lean toward the model.
  • A single card is noisy. One pick can go either way for reasons no model or front office could see coming. The seven-class mean is the real test; the individual cards are how you read it.
  • How WAR is measured here: Realized WAR to date: y_early_war from the answers files, summed over the player's NBA seasons scored so far, at most five (2019-2021 classes have complete five-season windows; 2022-2025 are partial and still accruing, so compare within a draft year, not across). Players who never appeared in the NBA score 0. A handful of draft-and-stash picks have no pre-draft or outcome record in our data at all; they are shown with WAR 0 and flagged in team_cards_audit.csv (war_note).

How DraftDB works

01
Only what was knowable before draft night

Every input is dated before the pick: college and international box scores, physical measurements, the dated mock-draft consensus and published scouting grades, and biography. Nothing from a player’s professional career reaches the model.

02
Trained on the 2000–2018 classes

The model learns from the drafts of 2000 through 2018. The classes on this page were never part of that training, and no draft’s own year or later is ever visible to it.

03
A gate every change has to pass

Any change to the model must clear a seven-fold walk-forward gate on 2012–2018 and then repeat the result under shifted random seeds before its blind years are allowed to be scored.

04
Blind classes, sealed vault

The 2019–2025 classes are blind. They are scored through a sealed vault using three seed sets, so the years on this page cannot be tuned against.

05
What the accuracy actually is

Verified order accuracy is 50.6% on expanding boards against 24.0% for the real draft order; on strict boards it is 41.8% against 26.3%. This page was scored on expanding boards.

06
Open to check

Everything is versioned on GitHub: the site and model documentation at Spoofyy-1/draftdb (docs/EXPERIMENTS.md, model/CURRENT_MODEL.json, and the board itself in model/board_current_2019_2025.csv), and the collected pre-draft data and its collectors at Spoofyy-1/DraftDB-Data.

Model on this page: EVO gen11 + international-line mask + NBADraft.net grades + Torvik league context + ridge on top columns. Boards used: expanding.
